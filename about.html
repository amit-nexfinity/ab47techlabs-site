About us

Making AI simple, useful and measurable.

AB47 Tech Labs Private Limited builds AI products that help businesses grow revenue and get more done, from independent owners to large franchises and brands. Nexfinity is our first product suite.

Our story

Lots of AI promises. Very few results.

Every business has been told AI will change everything. Most have tried a tool or two, and very few can point to the revenue it earned or the hours it saved. The technology is real. What's missing are products that are simple to adopt and built around business outcomes.

We started AB47 Tech Labs in 2026 to build those products. Our rule is simple: if the impact doesn't show up in revenue or productivity, it isn't worth building.

We began with industries where customer experience drives growth: restaurants, hotels, salons and clinics, from single outlets to large multi-location franchises. Nexfinity is the result: AI agents and software for reviews, AI search visibility and daily operations.
